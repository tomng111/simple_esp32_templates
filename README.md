# HiveMQ - ESP32
HiveMQ - ESP32.<br />
## Installation<br /> 
- <YOUR_WIFI_SSID> - Your own WIFI SSID<br />
- <YOUR_WIFI_PASS> - Your own WIFI password<br />
- <YOUR_URL @ OVERVIEW> - Overview/Connection Details/URL<br />
- <YOUR_WEBSOCKETPORT @ OVERVIEW> - Overview/Connection Details/WebSocket Port<br />
- <YOUR_USERNAME @ WEBCLIENT> - Web Client/Connection Settings/Username<br />
- <YOUR_PASS @ WEBCLIENT> - Web Client/Connection Settings/Password<br />
## Send Message<br /> 
- `{"led1":1, "led2":1}` - Turn LED1 and LED2 on<br />
- `{"led1":1}` - Turn LED1 on<br />
- `{"led2":1}` - Turn LED2 on<br />
- `{"led1":0, "led2":0}` - Turn LED1 and LED2 off<br />
- `{"led1":0}` - Turn LED1 off<br />
- `{"led2":0}` - Turn LED2 off<br />
